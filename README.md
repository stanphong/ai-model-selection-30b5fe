# Prompt, RAG, or Fine-Tune? (Pick the Right Lever)

An AI-Fluency project about the highest-leverage decision in applied AI — prompt vs RAG vs fine-tune vs right-sizing the model — made on cost, quality, latency, and maintenance rather than fashion. You start from the ordinary way teams choose an approach (whatever is trendy, whatever a confident assistant recommends) and work through three mini-scenarios as the project grows: a fresh-facts problem where RAG is right and fine-tuning bakes in stale facts, a narrow-stable-format-at-scale problem where a small fine-tune beats a brittle mega-prompt on cost and latency, and a within-a-strong-model problem where the right move is just to prompt and not over-engineer. For each scenario you read the requirement, see a confidently-wrong AI recommend the wrong lever, pick the right one WITH justification tied to the binding constraint, and prove it with a sketched eval and a quick cost model. Taught in a Claude-Code-style terminal — rehearsed commands and full agentic traces on one side, a tutor and a curious student on the other. A read-only walkthrough; you watch a fashion-driven choice become a constraint-driven, eval-proven one and answer understanding checks, including why a confident AI recommendation is not a justification.

Built step-by-step with [KhwajaLabs Build](https://khwajalabs.com).
